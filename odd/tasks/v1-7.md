## Specs

- S1 Owner (verbatim): "M'agradaria que fosin frases motivacionals, curtes." Owner selection (exact): "Re-curar a curt + motivacional, 1.000 per idioma". The corpora must hold motivational, short lines. Owner selection (L38, exact): "Tancar la v1.7 amb unes 700 per idioma (Recomanat)": the same number of lines in each language, about 700 (was 1,000).
- S2 Length rule: the `text` field, trimmed, is at most 60 characters. The quote view on the reference TV is 404 dp wide at 16 sp (about 50 characters per line, two lines allowed), so 60 characters keeps every line inside the space reserved for it and never needs the ellipsis. The old 160-character bound is dropped.
- S3 Tone rule: a line qualifies only if its point is encouraging, urging or life-affirming (effort, hope, courage, patience, learning, kindness, self-belief, resilience, curiosity, a positive truth). A line is rejected when its point is a warning, a prediction of misfortune, a piece of advice to be careful, a plain description of an everyday fact or custom, mockery or irony, or fatalism. It must also stand alone, without context, under the date.
  - Accepted examples: "El sólo querer es medio poder.", "No hay mayor dificultad que la poca voluntad.", "Date prisa, pero no corras.", "El entusiasmo es una aurora que no se termina nunca.", "Don't try to be someone you're not.", "The dogs bark but the caravan passes on.", "Kindness, like a boomerang, always returns.", "Truth may be blamed, but it shall never be shamed.", "Deixa fer vent, que la música és de qui l'entén.", "Treballs amb pa són bons de passar."
  - Rejected examples (the point is not encouragement): "Qui té cinc dits a les mans, ja pot portar guants.", "Ocell de bec ample, menja poc i molt escampa.", "Trons a la mar, aigua a la terra.", "Buena es la nieve que en su tiempo viene.", "Hasta el saber cansa, cuando es el saber por oficio.", "Arrieros somos y en el camino andamos.", "Still water runs deep.", "As proud as a peacock.", "There's no fool like an old fool.", "It is the early bird that gets the worm.", "All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy."
  - The rejected lines of the current corpora are pinned in the shared test, so a curation that reintroduces them fails.
- S4 Provenance rules (unchanged): `text<TAB>author<TAB>source[<TAB>translator]`; the author is empty for a traditional saying; every author died before 1956 (public domain in the EU, life + 70); a translator is a public-domain translator (named, died before 1956 or a pre-1900 edition) or `LlarTV` for a translation made for this project; `source` is the page the line was checked against, and for an `LlarTV` translation the original-language page it was translated from, never a modern translation. No modern copyrighted quote, no invented attribution.
- S5 Corpus shape: the same count in every language, about 700 (L38; was exactly 1,000); the sayings share stays between 25 % and 75 %; no author carries more than 10 lines; no duplicate texts (near-duplicates count as duplicates); the Catalan lines keep the current IEC spelling normalisation, and the wording of a restored public-domain line is not changed.
- S6 The app does not change its behaviour: the quote of the day is still the corpus line at `floorMod(epochDay, size)` (size is now about 700), the same line all day, in the interface language, with the night palette and the `quote` setting. The only code change is the version: `versionCode 8`, `versionName '1.7'`. The shared test tightens the length bound to 60 characters and adds the pinned rejected lines.
- S7 Documentation: `docs/quotes.md` records the tone rule, the length rule and how the corpora were curated (with the licence and the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution); the README keeps its quotes section and configuration table; the CHANGELOG gets a `[1.7]` section; `docs/performance/v1.7.md` records the paired emulator and reference-TV measurements.
